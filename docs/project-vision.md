# Project Vision

## Purpose

Quick Trends Analysis exists to reduce the amount of manual research required before analyzing basketball betting markets.

The objective is to automate repetitive statistical analysis while leaving the final betting decision to the user.

---

## Problem

Current research requires users to manually:

* Search previous games
* Calculate historical averages
* Compare home and away performance
* Evaluate recent trends

This process is repetitive and time-consuming.

---

## Solution

Allow users to select:

* League
* Teams
* Market
* Betting line

The application gathers the required historical data, applies the project's trend rules, and presents the results in a consistent format.

---

## Version 1 Scope

Version 1 intentionally remains small.

Supported:

* NBA
* Historical trend analysis
* Team-based markets
* Objective trend calculations

Not included:

* AI predictions
* Betting recommendations
* User accounts
* Saved picks
* Notifications
* Payment systems

The focus is building a reliable engineering foundation before expanding functionality.

---

## Long-Term Vision

The architecture should allow future expansion without major redesign.

Possible future additions include:

* Additional leagues
* More betting markets
* Historical databases
* Multiple data providers
* Advanced statistical models

The architecture should remain closed to modification but open for extension wherever practical.
